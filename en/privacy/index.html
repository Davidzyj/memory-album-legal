Privacy Policy

Memory Album Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026. Memory Album is a local template-based video creation app. In the current version, core processing happens on your device.

No account required No ad tracking Local-first creation

How We Handle Your Data

1. Purposes

  • Read the photos and videos you choose to fill templates and create memory videos.
  • Save generated videos back to the system Photos library.
  • Save recent projects and editing progress locally on your device so you can continue later.

2. Permissions Used

  • Photo Library read access: used to read the photos and videos you choose.
  • Photo Library write access: used to save generated videos back to Photos.

3. Photo and Video Uploads

The current version does not upload the photos, videos, or generated results you choose to developer servers. It also does not require account registration or collect your phone number, name, or email for the main creation flow.

4. Local Data

  • Draft files and editing progress
  • Media identifiers referenced by recent projects
  • Basic interface state and app preferences

This information is used only to continue editing, restore projects, and provide the basic app experience. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Third-Party Components

  • IQKeyboardManager
  • Masonry
  • YYModel

These components are used for input behavior, layout, and model conversion. They do not provide advertising, account, or analytics features in the current app.

6. Policy Updates

If future versions add cloud sync, online assets, cloud-based AI generation, account login, or diagnostics, we will update this policy before those features are released and reassess App Store privacy disclosures.
